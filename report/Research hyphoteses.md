# Methods - Main Goal and Specific Objectives

## Main Goal

The primary objective of this study is to evaluate and compare non-destructive methods for estimating the modulus of elasticity (MOE) in wood, specifically assessing the performance of Resistograph (micro-drilling) and acoustic tools (TreeSonic and Microsecond Timer) against reference MOE measurements. The study aims to quantify the accuracy, agreement, and bias of each method and to determine their suitability for operational forestry and wood quality assessment.

## Specific Objectives

Specifically:

1) Compare the MOE values between destructive vs. non-destructive measures (GLM model).
2) Compare agreement among methods using Bland–Altman analysis (Bland-Altman plots).
3) Assess prediction performance using RMSE, bias, and percent bias.
